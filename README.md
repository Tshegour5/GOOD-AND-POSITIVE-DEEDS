# GOOD-AND-POSITIVE-DEEDS
Good and positive deeds is a non-governmental organization (NGO) dedicated to providing a safe, caring, and supportive environment for elderly individuals in the community. Our goal is to improve the quality of life for senior citizens by offering essential services, emotional support, and a sense of belonging. 
Mission statement: To provide compassionate, affordable, and high quality residential care for our elderly, ensuring their physical, emotional, and social well-being.
Vission statement: To build a community where every elderly person is valued, respected, and able to live with dignity, comfort, and security.
Target audience: elderly people who are aged 60 and above, seniors without family or financial support, families seeking quality care for elder;y relatives, donors, volunteers, and community partners.
Services offered: Safe and comfortable accomodation, nutritious daily meals, basic healthcare cupport and regular check-ups, recreational and social activities, emotional and psychological support.
How to get involved: Donate funds or resources, volunteer your time and skills, partner with us as a sponsor or service provider, help spread awareness.
Goals: Provide safe housing for elderly residents, build strong community relationships, ensure sustainable funding and growth
Our promise: We  are committed to transparency, accountability, and delivering the highest standards of care. Good and positive deeds strives to be a trusted home where our elders can live peacefully and with dignity.
